# Journeys

Walk these as a stranger. You should not need a tour. Start each one from the door unless it says otherwise.

## 1. Raise a supplier invoice and send it

You are Mara. A bale of bookcloth arrived with a bill.

1. Click **Mara Ellison**.
2. Click **Raise a slip**.
3. Supplier: **Wick & Twine**. Their number: `WT-1102`. Issued: today.
4. On the first line, describe `Forest buckram, 20 yards`. Qty `20`. Unit `18.50`.
5. Link **PO-502** (or press **Use PO-502** on the notepad).
6. The stamp on the line should read **Matched**. **Send to the books**.
7. The paper stamps **Submitted**. Back to the blotter: the slip sits under *Already moving*.

Success: a new supplier invoice exists, with line items, in `submitted`.

## 2. Finish yesterday's draft

Helios started a binding invoice and left the purchase order blank.

1. From the blotter, open **Helios Bindery** (`HB-8891`).
2. Read the paper: 200 copies of Tide Tables at $12.50.
3. On the notepad, under unmatched lines, click **Use PO-311**.
4. Send to the books.

Success: the draft leaves "On the blotter" because it no longer needs you.

## 3. Fix a rejection

Northline billed twelve reams. PO-204 only has four left.

1. Open **Northline Paper Co.** (`NP-44102`).
2. Read the red rejection on the notepad.
3. Click **Split across open orders**.
4. Two lines appear: four reams on PO-204, eight on PO-218, both matched.
5. Send to the books.

Success: a rejected slip is corrected via its line items and resubmitted.

## 4. Settle an approved invoice

Finance already approved Calder's freight. The cartons are on the dock.

1. Open **Calder Freight** (`CF-77-190`).
2. The paper is read-only. Related work is receipt.
3. Check **Freight · 14 cartons**. Optionally write `On the dock at Powell's`.
4. Click **I received this**.

Success: the approved invoice is settled. It is released for payment and no longer waiting on Mara.

## 5. Answer a dispute

Harbor Type billed the 2024 license rate.

1. Open **Harbor Type Foundry** (`HT-2026-R`).
2. Read the argument on the notepad.
3. Either **Stand by this slip** (it returns to the books as submitted) or **Withdraw to draft** and change the unit amount to `480.00` against PO-117, then send.

Success: the disputed record is answered from related work, not from a separate queue.

## 6. Read a record that is already moving

1. Open **Meridian Inks** (submitted) and notice you cannot edit. The notepad is quiet.
2. Open **Northline** `NP-43880` (partially paid) and read the payment bar.
3. Open **Wick & Twine** `WT-1094` (paid). It is closed.

Success: every lifecycle the brief named can be seen as a record with its line items, without a second list screen.
