# Journeys

Walk these as a stranger. No tour is required; the blotter is the next paper.

## 1. Sit and remit the one that is due today

Sit as Finance Operator. **INV-8841 Helion Glass Works** is already on the blotter, $18,440.00, due this sitting. Leave the amount and ACH as they are. Press **Record remittance**. The veil says the rail is posting. A carmine **PAID** stamp lands. Helion leaves Ready. Northwind comes forward on its own.

You have gotten through the first outlay of the day.

## 2. Pay a remainder that was already started

From Ready, pick **Vale Packaging INV-8766**. The paper already shows $4,000 remitted on 22 Aug and $7,900 due — overdue. Record remittance for the remainder. It stamps **PAID**. Vale joins Stamped this sitting, under Bramble from the morning.

## 3. A bank return, then a retry

On **Northwind Timber**, press **Bank returned this attempt**. The attempt logs as failed (ACH unable to locate account). Status stays approved; the paper does not leave Ready. Change the rail to **Wire** if you like, and **Record remittance**. The retry succeeds. Northwind is paid.

## 4. Work the steel dispute, then pay what is left

Open **Open disputes**. **Meridian Steel INV-8712** is on the blotter with Elena Voss’s claim and $6,400 in contention. Leave the credit amount as filled. Optionally write a note. Press **Accept credit, return to ready**. The premium comes off; remainder is $36,200; the paper is now in Ready to remit with a **CREDITED** stamp. Record remittance. Meridian is paid.

## 5. Stand a duplicate down

Open disputes (or stay there after Meridian has left). **Osprey Inks INV-8690** claims to be a copy of INV-8520. Press **Stand the invoice down**. Status becomes rejected. Stamp **STOOD DOWN**. It appears under Stamped this sitting. Harrow will not pay it.

## 6. Send a dispute back as unfounded, then remit

If you still have Meridian open and would rather not credit: write a note that mill certs were superseded, press **Unfounded — pay as approved**. Meridian returns to Ready at the full $42,600. Remit it like any other approved invoice.

## 7. Clear the desk

Remit Helion, Northwind, Calder, and Vale. Resolve both disputes (credit or stand down or unfounded-and-pay). When both working trays read 0, the blotter says **The desk is clear.** Stamped this sitting holds the day’s paper.
