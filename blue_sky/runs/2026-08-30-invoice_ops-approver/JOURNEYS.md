# Journeys

Walk these as a stranger. No tour is required; the desk should make the next move obvious.

## 1. Sit down and reject the top sheet

The largest invoice is waiting, and it does not fully match a purchase order.

1. Open the app. Read “The work is on the desk.”
2. Click **Sit down as Checker**.
3. The stack is sorted by amount. **Helios Freight Co. · $48,220.00** is on top, marked unmatched.
4. Click **Pick up the top sheet**.
5. Read the status strip (**Submitted · awaiting stamp**), the supplier, the amount, and the three lines. Detention has no PO.
6. Click **Reject**. Choose **Unmatched to purchase order**. Add a short note if you like.
7. Click **Stamp reject**. The sheet is returned. The next one comes up the stack.

## 2. Approve a clean match

Not everything on the desk is a fight.

1. Sit down as Checker.
2. Skip the top sheet if you want — pick up **Northglass Studio · $12,850.50**.
3. All three lines are matched to PO-8744.
4. Click **Approve**. The copy says settlement happens later.
5. Click **Stamp approve** (a note is optional).
6. The invoice is released for settlement. You never see a payment screen.

## 3. Try to wave through a partial match

Kite & Copper billed more than the PO allowed.

1. Sit down and pick up **Kite & Copper · $6,040.00**.
2. Two lines are **Partial**. Read the notes: extra bumpers, sample kit over the cap.
3. Click **Approve**, then **Stamp approve** with the note left blank.
4. The desk refuses: you must say why an exception is acceptable.
5. Write a note (or go back and **Reject** instead with **Amount does not agree**).

## 4. Clear the whole stack

How an approver gets through the work in front of them today.

1. Sit down. Always pick up the top sheet, inspect, stamp, and let the next sheet arrive.
2. Helios (unmatched) → Northglass (matched) → Kite & Copper (partial) → Harbor Bindery (no PO required).
3. After the fourth stamp the desk goes quiet: **Nothing waiting.**
4. Today’s stamps are listed. Approved files are released for settlement.

## 5. Harbor Bindery — no purchase order on purpose

A retainer vendor is not a miss.

1. Sit down. Pick up **Harbor Bindery · $2,175.00** (bottom of the stack).
2. Lines are tagged **No PO**, not unmatched. The note says standing retainer.
3. Approve it. No exception note is required.
4. Put it back first if you want to feel that a stamp is a choice, not a load.

## 6. Re-read a stamp, then stand up

1. After at least one stamp, use **Today’s stamps** from the stack (or finish the stack).
2. Open a released or returned invoice. The status strip has changed. The reason is on the record. There is no second stamp.
3. An approved record reminds you that **settlement is the next human step**.
4. **Mara Chen · stand up** in the top right. Sit down again: the same work is still gone. Refresh the browser to restore the morning stack.
