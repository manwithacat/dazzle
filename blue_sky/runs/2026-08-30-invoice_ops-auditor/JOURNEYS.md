# Journeys

Walk these as a stranger. No tour required — the desk states the rule once: each slip triple-opens, trail order is attempt → invoice → tenant.

## 1. The NSF that is still live

The primary job.

1. Enter as Auditor.
2. On the ribbon, find **14:22 · PA-8841** (failed, NSF, Orchard & Co.).
3. Open the **attempt** id.
4. Read sheet 1: second try, retryable NSF, afternoon queue.
5. Read sheet 2: **INV-2044** is still **approved**. Settle Approved Invoice is waiting on **PA-8842**.
6. Notice **PA-8820** (29 Aug) on the invoice — yesterday’s try, not on today’s desk.
7. Read sheet 3: **T-ORCH**, account ****4412, harvest-season NSF note.
8. Cut folio for the file. Download or print.

You have traced a payment attempt back to the invoice without leaving the trail.

## 2. Triple-open from the invoice number

Same trail, different door.

1. From the desk, on the PA-8841 slip, open **INV-2044** (the middle file label).
2. The folio lands on the invoice sheet, already highlighted. Sheets 1 and 3 are still there, above and below.
3. Spine at the top still reads `PA-8841 traces to INV-2044 on T-ORCH`.

## 3. Triple-open from the tenant

1. From the desk, on any Orchard slip, open **T-ORCH**.
2. Land on the tenant sheet. The invoice book snapshot shows the full lifecycle on these books: draft, submitted, approved, paid, rejected — with **INV-2044** highlighted.
3. Scroll up to see which attempt you came from.

## 4. Split settlement, partially paid

1. Open **PA-8801** (succeeded wire, $30,000, Kite Harbor).
2. Invoice **INV-2010** is **partially_paid** for $48,000.
3. The sibling strip shows **PA-8802** pending ACH $18,000 — the remainder, same morning batch.
4. Switch to PA-8802 from the invoice. Same invoice, same tenant, other try.

## 5. Failed attempt, disputed invoice

1. Open **PA-8712** (failed, ACCOUNT_CLOSED).
2. Decline is not retryable.
3. Invoice **INV-1991** is **disputed**. Settle Approved Invoice was abandoned after one try.
4. Tenant **T-HALC** notes that non-retryable declines do not sit in approved.

## 6. A clean post, still cut for the file

1. Open **PA-8790** or **PA-8850**.
2. Succeeded. Invoice **paid**. Procedure complete.
3. Cut the folio anyway — the desk is an audit file, not an exception queue.

## 7. Work through the pile

1. Cut two or three trails.
2. Return to the desk. Those slips carry **Cut for the file**.
3. The rail counts `n of 7` cut. That is how an auditor gets through the work in front of them today.
